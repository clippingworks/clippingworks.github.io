Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Clip Poster is a private desktop tool. Its owner uses it to send short videos they edited from their own computer to TikTok accounts they own and operate, as drafts, using TikTok's Content Posting API. Only accounts operated by the owner are connected.

What data the tool accesses

  • Basic TikTok profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) for each connected account via the user.info.basic scope, only to confirm which account a clip is going to.
  • Permission to upload videos as drafts via the video.upload scope.

How data is stored

  • Access tokens are stored only in files on the owner's own computer, one per connected account. They are never sent anywhere except to TikTok's API.
  • Videos uploaded are the owner's own files from the owner's computer.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • We do not access any account that the owner does not operate. The tool is not available to the public.

Your choices

  • Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.
  • Deleting an account's local token file removes all data stored for that account.
