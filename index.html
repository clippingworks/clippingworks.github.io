Clip Poster

Clip Poster is a private desktop tool. Its owner uses it to send short video clips they edited from their own computer to the TikTok and YouTube accounts they own and run.

On TikTok, clips are sent as drafts through TikTok's Content Posting API. The account holder then reviews each draft in the TikTok app and posts it.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only accounts operated by the owner are connected.
